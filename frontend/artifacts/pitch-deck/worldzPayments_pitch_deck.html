06 / Why the payment model matters
VERIFICATION IS THE PRODUCT BOUNDARY

Submitted is not
settled.

The service verifies successful payout legs before presenting a payment as complete.

01Payer wallet

Signs a payer-bound order authorization.

→
02NEAR router

Executes merchant and applicable fee payouts asynchronously.

→
03Merchant service

Reconciles finalized events and router state.

→
04Payment settled

Verified settlement receipt issued.

Recovery without another payment

Partial and retryable payout states remain visible for reconciliation.

Proof beyond a browser redirect

Signed webhooks support server-side, idempotent fulfilment.

Order funds can remain in the routing contract while payouts complete. No custody of merchant wallet keys.
