# worldzPayments product roadmap

Updated: 6 November 2026

## Core product priorities

1. **Pilot and harden:** validate wallet-to-payout journeys, reconciliation, monitoring and merchant support.
2. **Package for builders:** publish the SDK and complete authenticated server integrations and easier checkout embedding.
3. **Deepen merchant operations:** expand invoice workflows, fulfilment connectors and reporting.

## Planned checkout integrations

| Integration | Intended buyer experience | Engineering estimate and dependencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shopify checkout payment app** | NEAR appears as a payment method inside Shopify checkout. | **4–8+ weeks of engineering**, plus Shopify partner access, review and approval time. |
| **Walletless card/bank checkout** | Buyer pays with fiat and never connects a NEAR wallet. | **Several more weeks**, depending on the payment provider, custody/settlement model and approvals. |

## Planned mobile app

**Platforms:** iOS and Android.

**Proposed merchant features:**

- Create and share payment links with item or service descriptions.
- Track payment status, settlement and payment history.
- View and access customer receipts.
- Receive payment notifications.
- Connect a compatible NEAR wallet for merchant access.

**Scope and timeline:** to be defined after product discovery. Dependencies include NEAR wallet connectivity, notification delivery, platform release requirements and parity with the current merchant platform.

The mobile app is a separate workstream from walletless fiat checkout.

All roadmap additions are planned. Current hosted checkout requires a NEAR wallet.

These are planning estimates rather than committed delivery dates. External approval time is additional to engineering time. Walletless checkout scope and timing depend on provider selection and the agreed custody, settlement and approval requirements.
